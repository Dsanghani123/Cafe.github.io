SHOPIFY FAQ

Technical Questions, Clearly Answered.

No confusing technical language. Just straightforward answers about how we approach Shopify development, performance and ongoing improvements.

Can you work with our existing Shopify theme?
Yes. We can work with an existing Shopify theme to improve its design, functionality, performance or structure. After reviewing the current implementation, we'll recommend whether it makes more sense to optimize the existing theme or rebuild specific areas.
Can you build a completely custom Shopify store?
Yes. We can build custom Shopify storefronts around your brand, customer journey and business requirements — from custom sections and templates to advanced storefront functionality.
Can you redesign our Shopify store without rebuilding everything?
Yes. Depending on the current theme and technical structure, we can redesign and improve selected areas without replacing the entire store. We'll first assess what should be retained, improved or rebuilt.
Can you improve our Shopify store speed?
Yes. We can review theme code, third-party scripts, apps, images and storefront functionality to identify potential performance bottlenecks and improvement opportunities. We focus on improving real storefront performance rather than chasing a score alone.
Can you work with our UI/UX designer?
Absolutely. Our development team can work directly with your existing designer or design team. We can review Figma files, clarify interactions and translate the approved experience into Shopify.
Can you build custom Shopify functionality?
Yes. We can develop custom Liquid sections, storefront components, theme functionality and integrations based on your requirements.
Can you migrate our existing store to Shopify?
Yes. We can support Shopify migrations including store structure, products, collections, URLs, redirects, theme development, integrations and pre-launch QA.
Do you provide ongoing Shopify development support?
Yes. We can support ongoing improvements, feature development, bug fixes, optimization and technical maintenance after launch.
