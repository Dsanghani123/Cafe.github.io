COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with our senior duo studio.

How does working with a 2-person studio compare to a traditional agency?
You get direct, daily collaboration with the actual senior designer and senior developer building your store. There are zero account managers, zero Junior handoffs, and zero communication overhead. This results in faster turnaround times, higher code quality, and significantly lower costs.
What is your typical project timeline?
Full custom store design and Liquid builds typically take 3 to 5 weeks from kickoff to launch. CRO redesigns and speed optimization projects usually take 1 to 2 weeks.
Do you build with custom Liquid or off-the-shelf themes?
We build bespoke, modular Shopify 2.0 Liquid themes from scratch or tailor high-performance foundations like Shopify Dawn. Every section is completely customisable in the Shopify Theme Editor without touching code.
Do you provide post-launch support and warranty?
Yes! Every store launch includes 30 days of dedicated bug-fix support and QA guarantee. We also offer ongoing monthly growth retainer partnerships for continuous CRO and feature development.
