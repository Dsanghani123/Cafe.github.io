COMMON QUESTIONS

Shopify Development FAQs

Everything you need to know about our engineering standards.

Will my marketing team be able to customize pages without coding?

Yes, 100%. Every section and component we build is hooked into Shopify's native Theme Customizer schemas with drag-and-drop controls for text, imagery, products, colors, and layout ordering.

How do you guarantee store speed during development?

We build with a performance-first mindset: critical CSS inlining, responsive WebP image source sets, deferred JavaScript loading, and zero unnecessary third-party tracking scripts.

Do you support Shopify Plus and Checkout Extensibility?

Yes. We develop custom checkout UI extensions, post-purchase upsell widgets, and Shopify Functions (custom discounts, delivery customizations, payment reordering) for Shopify Plus merchants.
