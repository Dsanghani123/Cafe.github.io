DATA QUESTIONS

Tracking & Analytics FAQs

Everything you need to know about setting up accurate data pipelines.

Why is browser-only tracking no longer enough?

Browser tracking relies on client-side cookies which are blocked by iOS Safari (ITP), Brave, Firefox, and ad-blocking extensions. Server-side tracking routes data directly from your server to Meta and Google, capturing 99%+ of all purchases.

Will this help lower my Meta and Google Ads CPA?

Yes! By feeding complete conversion signals back to Meta and Google AI bidding algorithms, your campaigns optimize for higher-value purchasers and eliminate blind ad spend.
