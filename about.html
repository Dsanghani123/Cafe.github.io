WHO WE ARE

Small Team. Focused Expertise. Bigger Thinking.

We started with a simple belief: building a great Shopify store shouldn't require choosing between beautiful design and solid development.

Too often, design happens in one place, development happens somewhere else, and the original experience gets lost somewhere in between. We work differently.

At Kinetic Duo Studio, UI/UX designers and Shopify developers work closely from the beginning. We think about how an experience should look, how customers will use it, how Shopify will support it and how the final store can perform in the real world.

The result is a more connected process — and a better experience for both the brand and its customers.

"Design with purpose. Development with intention. Every decision connected."
