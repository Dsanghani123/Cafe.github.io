TACTICAL AUDITS

Recent Field Observations

Structured in our 4-part framework: Observation, Why it matters, What to check, and Practical recommendation.

MOBILE UX & INTERACTION
Sticky Mobile Add-to-Cart & Widget Occlusion
Insight #01
01 / Observation
Floating live-chat badges, cookie banners, and sticky bottom CTA bars colliding at the bottom 120px of mobile viewports.
02 / Why It Matters
Creates rage-clicks and accidental widget opens when shoppers try to tap "Add to Cart" while reading product reviews.
03 / What To Check
Open your PDP on iPhone Safari, scroll to reviews, and verify if the chat launcher obscures the primary buy button.
04 / Practical Fix
Manage z-index layers; programmatically hide the floating chat icon whenever the sticky bottom checkout bar engages.
CORE WEB VITALS
Variant Selection Layout Shift (CLS)
Insight #02
01 / Observation
Switching product size/color swatch causes the gallery height to collapse and expand abruptly while fetching image assets.
02 / Why It Matters
Causes jarring Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS), mis-taps on mobile buttons, and lowers organic SEO rankings on Google.
03 / What To Check
Inspect PDP in Chrome DevTools with 4G throttling; click color variants and monitor the CLS score in the Experience tab.
04 / Practical Fix
Define explicit CSS aspect-ratio: 1/1 on all image containers and preload adjacent variant media arrays.
CRO & AOV EXPANSION
Cart Drawer Free Shipping Threshold Friction
Insight #03
01 / Observation
Displaying "Add $14 more for free shipping" progress bar without providing 1-click add-on items inside the slide-cart.
02 / Why It Matters
Customers want free shipping, but navigating back to search or collection pages creates friction and cart abandonment.
03 / What To Check
Analyze GA4 drop-off from cart view to checkout start when cart value is within $10-$20 of your free shipping threshold.
04 / Practical Fix
Embed dynamic sub-$20 quick-add product recommendations right beneath the shipping progress bar inside the drawer.
PERFORMANCE & SCRIPTS
Third-Party Review Widget Script Bloat
Insight #04
01 / Observation
Review and loyalty apps injecting 400kb+ of uncompressed JavaScript synchronously in the document head before render.
02 / Why It Matters
Blocks main thread execution, delaying Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) and degrading mobile Interaction to Next Paint (INP).
03 / What To Check
Run WebPageTest or PageSpeed Insights; inspect the network waterfall for third-party scripts executing before DOMContentLoaded.
04 / Practical Fix
Lazy-load review widgets using IntersectionObserver so review scripts only load when scrolled within 400px of view.
CONVERSION DESIGN
Auto-Advancing Homepage Carousels
Insight #05
01 / Observation
Homepage hero carousels automatically sliding every 3 seconds on mobile without user gesture or interaction.
02 / Why It Matters
Causes banner blindness and frustration; industry data shows less than 2% of mobile shoppers ever click past slide 1.
03 / What To Check
Review Microsoft Clarity or Hotjar click maps and scroll heatmaps on your homepage hero section across mobile devices.
04 / Practical Fix
Replace the carousel with a static, clear value proposition and a 4-pillar quick-category navigation grid.
