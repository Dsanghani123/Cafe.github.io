Product Detail Page (PDP) Architecture Comparison

✕ Standard Template (High Friction)
  • • Tiny add-to-cart button hidden below fold on mobile
  • • Vague product details hidden in long text walls
  • • Social proof placed at bottom of page where 80% drop off
  • • Confusing variant dropdowns that hide stock levels

  • • Instant slide-out cart drawer with free shipping progress bar
