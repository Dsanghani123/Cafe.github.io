UX STRATEGY

Good Design Looks Better. Great UX Works Better.

We don't design screens in isolation. We look at the complete customer journey — from the first interaction with your brand to product discovery, decision-making, cart and checkout.

01 • DISCOVER
Make the brand and value proposition immediately clear.
02 • EXPLORE
Help customers quickly find relevant products and categories.
03 • EVALUATE
Present the right product information at the right moment.
04 • DECIDE
Reduce uncertainty with clear CTAs, trust signals and useful information.
05 • CHECKOUT
Make the final steps simple, predictable and friction-free.
06 • RETURN
Create experiences that encourage customers to come back.
CRO-DRIVEN DESIGN

Pretty Design vs. Purposeful Design

A visually attractive store is only the starting point. We design every major interaction around clarity, usability and the actions you want customers to take.

Standard / Generic Design
  • ✕ Designed mainly around visual appearance
  • ✕ Important information buried below the fold
  • ✕ Navigation based on internal assumptions
  • ✕ Product pages overloaded with content
  • ✕ Generic calls-to-action
  • ✕ Mobile experience treated as an afterthought
Conversion-Focused UX
  • ✓ Clear value proposition from the first interaction
  • ✓ Strong information hierarchy
  • ✓ Navigation built around customer intent
  • ✓ Product pages structured around buying decisions
  • ✓ Clear and contextual CTAs
  • ✓ Mobile experience designed intentionally

DESIGN FAQ

Questions About Our Design Process?

Here are a few things clients usually ask before starting a UI/UX project.

Do you design Shopify stores from scratch?
Yes. We can design the complete Shopify experience from UX strategy and wireframes through high-fidelity UI design and developer-ready Figma files.
Can you redesign only specific pages?
Absolutely. We can focus on high-impact areas such as your homepage, product pages, collection pages, cart drawer or mobile experience.
Do you work with existing Shopify stores?
Yes. We can audit your existing experience and recommend improvements without requiring a complete redesign.
Do you provide Figma files?
Yes. Design projects can include organized Figma files with reusable components, responsive layouts and developer handoff documentation.
Can you work with our existing developer?
Yes. We can collaborate directly with your internal development team or external Shopify developer to ensure the design is implemented accurately.
Do you focus on conversion rate optimization?
Yes. Conversion thinking is part of our UX approach. We consider customer intent, information hierarchy, friction points, CTAs and shopping journeys when designing key pages.
Do you design mobile and desktop separately?
We design responsive experiences intentionally for both mobile and desktop rather than simply scaling one layout down.
