REDESIGN QUESTIONS

Store Redesign FAQs

Common questions about redesigning your store with Kinetic Duo Studio.

Will our customer accounts or order history be affected?

No. Your database of customer logins, order histories, gift cards, and product reviews remains 100% untouched. We are simply upgrading the visual theme and front-end code.

How do you protect our Google rankings?

We preserve URL slugs, implement automated 301 redirects for any modified paths, keep all meta tags, and build richer structured schema markup (JSON-LD) to improve search engine rankings.
